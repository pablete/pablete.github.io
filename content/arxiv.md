A few papers I've co-authored.

## 2026

**[DiffHDR: Re-Exposing LDR Videos with Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06161)**\
*Zhengming Yu, Li Ma, Mingming He, Leo Isikdogan, Yuancheng Xu, Dmitriy
Smirnov, Pablo Salamanca, Dao Mi, Pablo Delgado, Ning Yu, Julien Philip,
Xin Li, Wenping Wang, Paul Debevec.*

Frames LDR-to-HDR video conversion as a generative task in the latent space
of a video diffusion model, recovering plausible radiance lost to saturation
and quantization in 8-bit footage.

## 2023

**[InTune: Reinforcement Learning-based Data Pipeline Optimization for Deep Recommendation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08500)**\
*Kabir Nagrecha, Lingyi Liu, Pablo Delgado, Prasanna Padmanabhan.*

Addresses the data-ingestion bottleneck that often dominates training time
in modern DLRMs by using reinforcement learning to tune the data pipeline
itself.

## 2019

**[Gradient Boosted Decision Tree Neural Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09340)**\
*Mohammad Saberian, Pablo Delgado, Yves Raimond.*

A method to convert ensembles of decision trees into a single neural network
with relaxed properties, enabling end-to-end training and finer-grained
optimization than is possible with the original tree ensemble.
